About Me

I design. 🎨

I'm a final year B.Sc Computer Science student who got obsessed with one question: why do some websites feel effortless, while others make you want to close the tab?

That question pulled me into front-end development and UI/UX design at the same time. I taught myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript while obsessing over spacing, contrast, and why button placement matters.

Today I sit at the intersection most teams split between two people — I can wireframe a screen in Figma and build it in code the same afternoon.
